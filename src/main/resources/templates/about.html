About Stock Society

A clothing warehouse system designed to make product and inventory management easier.

Our Purpose

One place to manage clothing inventory

Stock Society is a web-based warehouse management application for clothing and apparel products. It allows users to add products, organize item details, and review saved records through a browser.

The project is based on a realistic e-commerce warehouse domain. Future versions can also support suppliers, customers, distribution centres, orders, shipments, and detailed stock records.

Product Organization

Organize apparel by brand, category, size, colour, supplier, and price.

Database Persistence

Save product records to a real database so information remains available after the application restarts.

Easy Product Search

Search, filter, sort, and browse warehouse records in a clean and readable list.
